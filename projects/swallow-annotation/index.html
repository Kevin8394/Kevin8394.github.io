Swallow Annotation

Dataset curation, marker verification, and time alignment for a large swallow study spanning MBSS and ambulatory patient sessions.

Project overview

The study combines clinician-facing Biopac recordings with wearable IMU data that can begin on different clocks. Reliable labels therefore depend on identifying shared signal landmarks, correcting offsets, and excluding ambiguous or incomplete sessions.

01

Role

Reviewed patient sessions, aligned Biopac and IMU streams using speech and calibration patterns, and verified 3,400 swallow markers against decimated microphone data across 62 Sibel patient datasets.

02

Contribution

Created a UTC-offset calculator from collection forms, documented dataset validity, and developed repeatable checks for clock skew, missing channels, and accidental marker presses.

03

Result

Contributed to a curated corpus of more than 300 MBSS and ambulatory sessions, with 3,400 verified markers across 62 patients feeding the 2026 detector work.
